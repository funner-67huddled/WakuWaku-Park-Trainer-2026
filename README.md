## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **WakuWaku Park**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life controls for WakuWaku Park's timed chase gameplay. It can help manage stamina, stage time, pursuer pressure, and movement while preserving the game's core objective of escaping each stage.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to experience WakuWaku Park, practice routes, reduce chase pressure, or replay its short timed stages without repeated failures.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through the park while monitoring stamina and the position of the pursuing enemies. Use the movement assist option when you need steadier control during a chase. Infinite stamina is most useful for maintaining distance, while slow pursuers gives you more time to change direction and avoid being cornered.

### Tools & Inventory

Iced coffee is the game's stamina-restoration resource. Use it when stamina begins to run low during a pursuit. The unlimited iced coffee option keeps this recovery tool available throughout the stage without changing the need to manage your route.

### World & Environment

WakuWaku Park is structured around three short stages, each lasting roughly three minutes. Enemy numbers increase from one stage to the next, so later sections create greater pursuit pressure. Freeze the stage timer when learning routes or inspecting the park, then disable it for a closer-to-normal challenge.

### Combat & Weapons

WakuWaku Park does not feature a conventional combat system. The core challenge is escape: run through the park, avoid the men in black, restore stamina with iced coffee, and reach safety before time expires. The trainer's defensive options reduce pursuit pressure rather than adding attacks or combat abilities.
